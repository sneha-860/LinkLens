## RQ5: Semantic layer and σ design (C5)

*Does the REF-gated hybrid σ recover real editorial links better than cosine alone, and how sensitive are the results to σ, ε and α?* Experiments: E6, E7.

**Headline (C5):** C5 not supported: no significant advantage of the hybrid over cosine (one-sided Holm p = 0.723 ≥ 0.05; MRR: mean difference +0.020, r = +0.17, n = 28 sites).

- **t5_e6_results** (E6, table) — Link-masking recovery. 10–20% of editorial links masked (links and anchors removed, REF and embeddings rebuilt), five repeats per site; each method's MRR, Recall@k and AUC, mean over sites [bootstrap 95% CI]. `tables/t5_e6_results.csv`, `tables/t5_e6_results.tex`
- **t5_e6_c5** (E6, table) — C5: hybrid against cosine. The refutation test for C5: the REF-gated hybrid against cosine across sites, Wilcoxon signed-rank one-sided (hybrid better) and two-sided, rank-biserial r, Holm over MRR (primary), R@10 and AUC. `tables/t5_e6_c5.csv`, `tables/t5_e6_c5.tex`
- **t5_e6_c5_by_class** (E6, table) — C5 per class. The C5 test within each class. `tables/t5_e6_c5_by_class.csv`, `tables/t5_e6_c5_by_class.tex`
- **f5_e6_methods** (E6, figure) — Recovery by method. Each method's E6 MRR, mean over sites with a bootstrap 95% CI; the random baseline dashed. `figures/f5_e6_methods.pdf`, `figures/f5_e6_methods.png`, `figures/f5_e6_methods.tex`
- **t5_e7_ablation** (E7, table) — σ ablation. Each σ variant at the default ε and α: top-k overlap with the default, E3 ΔPR and gain over random, and E6 recovery; mean over sites [bootstrap 95% CI], all sites and per class. `tables/t5_e7_ablation.csv`, `tables/t5_e7_ablation.tex`
- **t5_e7_sigma_pairs** (E7, table) — Top-k overlap between σ variants. The σ variants' pairwise top-k Jaccard at the default ε and α, mean over sites. `tables/t5_e7_sigma_pairs.csv`, `tables/t5_e7_sigma_pairs.tex`
- **t5_e7_scoring** (E7, table) — S against importance-weighted S_imp. Each σ at the default ε and α under S and under S_imp = S × importance(target) (L12, heuristic weights): E3 gain over random for the top-k, the difference, and S_imp's top-k overlap with the default; mean over sites [bootstrap 95% CI]. `tables/t5_e7_scoring.csv`, `tables/t5_e7_scoring.tex`
- **f5_e7_epsilon** (E7, figure) — Sensitivity to ε. Top-k overlap with the default, E3 gain over random and E6 MRR across the ε sweep, one line per σ; the default ε dashed. `figures/f5_e7_epsilon.pdf`, `figures/f5_e7_epsilon.png`, `figures/f5_e7_epsilon.tex`
- **f5_e7_alpha** (E7, figure) — Sensitivity to α. Top-k overlap with the default and E3 gain over random across the α sweep, one line per σ (α does not enter E6); the default α dashed. `figures/f5_e7_alpha.pdf`, `figures/f5_e7_alpha.png`, `figures/f5_e7_alpha.tex`
